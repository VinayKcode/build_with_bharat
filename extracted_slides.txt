PROBLEM STATEMENT
THE FIRST 60 MINUTES ARE SPENT WAITING - NOT HEALING
THE PROBLEM
India loses 1.77 lakh lives on its roads every year, and the deadliest gap is the first hour after a crash. A crash raises no alert by itself - the victim waits for a bystander to call 108. The control room sends an ambulance blind, with no view of which hospital has a free bed or trauma unit. The ambulance takes 20-30 minutes to arrive, one in four never does, and victims are often taken to a hospital that is already full - then driven on, losing 15-25 minutes at every retry. About 30% die before reaching any hospital at all.

PROPOSED SOLUTION
FROM CRASH TO HOSPITAL, IN ONE FLOW
GOLDEN HOUR is a crash-to-hospital coordination flow: the phone detects the crash by itself, the engine ranks hospitals that can actually treat the patient, and the ambulance, hospital and family are alerted at once.
01
DETECT
FIXES: 
the wait for a bystander to call
Phone sensors raise the alert by themselves
Confidence score filters false alarms
30-second cancel window for the user
02
DECIDE
FIXES: 
blind dispatch with no bed data
Rejects hospitals with no trauma/ICU/blood
Scores the rest: rating, beds, road ETA
Real road ETA via OSRM, not straight line
03
RESPOND
FIXES: 
transfers between full hospitals
Ambulance assigned to the chosen hospital
Hospital pre-alerted with patient ETA
Green corridor requested
Every step above replaces a phone call the victim cannot afford to wait for.
NOT INTEGRATED: 
Detect, rank and route run end to end on open data. Ambulance handover, hospital bed counts and green-corridor clearance are sent as requests to people, not live system integrations. Those need 108, hospital and traffic-police access, which this prototype does not have.
ROLLOUT HYPOTHESIS, NOT A CLAIM: 
a larger rollout would require access to government and hospital data systems, which this prototype does not have.

WHY WE BELIEVE IT
False crash detection
GPS speed-drop + accelerometer spike + 30 s cancel window
An accelerometer-only detector scores ROC-AUC 0.482 - worse than random. GPS-based scores 0.970.  [Liu et al., 2023]
Where does capability data come from
Curated, verified CSV + hospital-side admin panel
OpenStreetMap carries no ICU or trauma-level tag, so a human fills it in. The hospital enters its own state.
No internet in rural areas
Offline-first cache of last-known bed and capability state
108 is prescribed 20 min urban but 30 min rural and 40 min desert.  [CAG]
Nobody responds at the scene
Bystander mode
A nearby person shares the live case; the case is already ranked before they arrive.
Will the government integrate
Phase 2. The demo assumes no permission
eDAR / NTR data access needs MoRTH approval, so the prototype runs entirely on open data.
